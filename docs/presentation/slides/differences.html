Read the table row by row; each row's "limited by" is the motivation for the next row. All-pairs (#1–#3): the problem is between SMs, fixed by creating the right number of work items. The gain is 2.4–2.6× at small N and disappears once every SM has work. Trees (#4–#7): the dominant loss is inside the warp. Sorting is the necessary first step, the warp walk removes most of the rest. #6 and #8 are negative results with a mechanism, which is as useful as a speedup: they show the hardware scheduler and the GPU-resident design were already right. The bottom line is the story in one sentence; say it out loud. Speedups are best against best, tuned per variant, at θ = 0.5, Plummer initial condition, clean 0 % load runs.
