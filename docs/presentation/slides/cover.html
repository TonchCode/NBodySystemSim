Gravitational N-body on an RTX 4080 SUPER

From O(N²) direct summation to a warp-cooperative Barnes-Hut tree, and what limits each step

Toncho Nikolov

Technical University of Sofia, Plovdiv Branch · Faculty of Electronics and Automation · [Course name] · [Date]

Open with the one-line story: we simulate gravity between up to millions of bodies on one GPU, try eight load-balancing techniques, and explain with measurements why each one helped or did not. The headline result: a warp-cooperative tree walk is 5.8× faster than the sorted tree and 18× faster than the best CPU code at 1M bodies. Docs: README.md (build and run), docs/final/03–07. Study guide for the defence: docs/final/07-defence-guide.md.
