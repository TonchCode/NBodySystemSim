Say: this is the only physics in the code. Each interaction is about 20 floating-point operations and exactly one reciprocal square root, which runs on the special-function unit. That unit is 8 times slower than the FMA lanes, which is why it shows up later in the profiler. Point to make: every kernel, all-pairs and tree, calls exactly this function, so "one interaction" is the same work everywhere. That is what makes the speedups comparable. ε is the softening length: it stops the force from blowing up when two bodies get very close.
