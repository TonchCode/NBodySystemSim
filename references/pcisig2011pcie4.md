# pcisig2011pcie4 (STANDARDS BODY PRESS RELEASE, via reprint)

**Citation:** PCI-SIG. "PCI-SIG Announces PCI Express 4.0 Evolution to 16GT/s, Twice the Throughput of PCI Express 3.0 Technology". Press release, 29 November 2011. Reprinted by Design & Reuse.
**Link:** https://www.design-reuse.com/news/27909/pci-express-4-0.html
**Verified on:** 2026-10-17 (reprint fetched; headline, date and issuer confirmed). **pcisig.com was not fetched:** it served a JavaScript bot-check page, which we did not try to get around.
**Lead-group country:** USA (standards body).

## Key facts
- PCI-SIG approved "16 gigatransfers per second (GT/s) as the bit rate" for PCIe 4.0.
- 16 GT/s doubles the bandwidth of PCIe 3.0.
- The release does **not** state the encoding (see solomon2014pcie16gts) or a per-lane GB/s figure.

## Summary
1. The standards body's own announcement of the PCIe 4.0 bit rate.
2. It is a 2011 announcement, not the final 2017 specification, and it reached us as a verbatim reprint.
3. It gives the 16 GT/s per-lane rate.
4. Encoding comes from a separate source.
5. The final PCIe 4.0 spec itself is members-only and was not consulted.

## What it lets us claim
PCIe 4.0 signals at 16 GT/s per lane. With 128b/130b [solomon2014pcie16gts]: 16 × 128/130 / 8 ≈ 1.97 GB/s per lane per direction, so ×16 ≈ **31.5 GB/s per direction** [D]. The chapter's "~32 GB/s" is the rounded value.

## Quote
"16 gigatransfers per second (GT/s) as the bit rate" (PCI-SIG, 2011)
